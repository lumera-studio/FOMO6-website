How to Play

  1. Connect your wallet. Participation opens after launch.
  2. Each successful entry costs 20,000 $FOMO6, sent to the burn address.
  3. The first successful entry starts a 6-hour countdown.
  4. Each subsequent successful entry adds 120 seconds, capped at 6 hours remaining.
  5. When the timer expires, the current last player wins.
  6. The jackpot is funded by verified BNB token trading taxes received by the contract. Entry tokens are burned and do not enter the jackpot. A tax receipt after expiry settles the round and attempts to pay its existing jackpot directly to the winner. That receipt and taxes received between rounds fund the next jackpot. Anyone can also settle an expired round.
  7. After settlement, the next round waits for its first successful entry to start a new six-hour countdown. Unpaid prizes from previous rounds remain separate from the new jackpot.

Token approval is required before your first entry. The game runs in separate rounds. If automatic payment fails, only that round’s winner can claim the reserved prize, paid exclusively to the winner address. The planned Flap integration includes Guardian emergency recovery, which can withdraw the current jackpot and unpaid prizes and permanently stop all rounds and claims. Final contract details will be published before participation opens.
